# Combat Mechanics & Ruleset (Game Design Document)



## 1. Core Stats



Every combatant (Hero and Monsters) relies on four primary stats:

* HP (Health Points): Determines life. If HP reaches 0, the combatant is defeated.

* ATK (Attack): The offensive power used to calculate physical damage.

* DEF (Defense): Armor and mitigation used to resist physical damage.

* Speed: Governs hit accuracy, evasion, and the ability to flee from battle.



## 2. Turn Structure



* Phases: The Player always acts first, followed by the Enemy.

* Action Menu: The Player chooses between Attack, Item, Spell, or Run.

* Enemy Action: The Enemy rolls a 1d6 to determine their move from a 6-slot move table.



## 3. Accuracy & Hit Check (The 3d6 System)



Attacks do not automatically hit. Accuracy relies on a tabletop-style 3d6 bell curve compared against the combatants' Speeds.

* Formula: 3d6 + Attacker Speed vs 10 + Defender Speed

* Execution: To hit, the Attacker's roll must be Greater Than or Equal To (>=) the Defender's Target AC.

* Note: At equal speeds, 3d6 averages 10.5, resulting in a base hit chance of roughly 62.5%.



## 4. Damage Calculation



Damage is completely deterministic based on stats, with no random variance.

* Formula: Attacker ATK - Defender DEF

* Minimum Damage: Damage is always clamped to a minimum of 1 to ensure attacks never deal 0 damage.



## 5. Status Effects



Combatants can be afflicted by one status effect at a time (tracked as integers 1 through 4):

* Status 1 (Poison): Afflicted combatant takes 1d3 poison damage at the end of their turn.

* Status 2 (Sleep): Afflicted combatant rolls 1d6 at the start of their turn. On a 5 or 6, they wake up and act. On a 1 to 4, they remain asleep and skip their turn.

* Status 3 (Blind): Afflicted combatant suffers a -3 penalty to their 3d6 Accuracy Roll when attacking.

* Status 4 (Silence): Blocks the afflicted combatant from using the "Cast a Spell" command.



## 6. Fleeing Battle



Escaping a battle requires a standard 1d20 check based on Speed.

* Formula: Hero Speed + 1d20 > Monster Speed + 15

* Success: Battle ends immediately and returns to the overworld.

* Failure: Turn is wasted, and the Enemy gets to attack.



## 7. Enemy AI (Move Tables)



Enemies execute actions based on a 6-slot randomized move table (Move_1 through Move_6).

* Viper Example: Strikes on 1-5, spits Poison Spores on a 6.

* Trasgu Example: Strikes on 1-3, uses Screech on 4-5, Dances on a 6.
